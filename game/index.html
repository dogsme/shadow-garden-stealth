THE LANTERN GARDEN

a game of moonlight, hedges & held breath

Move WASD or Arrow keys  ·  Sneak hold Shift (slow, quiet, harder to spot)  ·  Restart R
Slip past the guards' lantern cones, hide in the bushes, steal all 3 moon-keys, then reach the glowing exit gate. If a guard sees you the detection meter fills — break line of sight and it drains. If it fills completely, the alarm sounds and the night begins again.
