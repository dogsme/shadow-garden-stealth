A top-down stealth game set in a walled garden at night. You play a small thief in a dark cloak: slip past three patrolling guards and their sweeping lantern cones, hide in the bushes when a cone turns your way, steal all three moon-keys, then reach the glowing exit gate unseen. Being spotted fills the detection meter — break line of sight and it drains — but if it fills completely the alarm sounds and the night starts over.

Move with WASD or arrow keys, hold Shift to sneak slowly and quietly, press R to restart. Pure canvas art and procedural WebAudio, no external assets, plays fully offline.
